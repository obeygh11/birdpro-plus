Important: This is an early prototype. Photo-based feed values and bird disease diagnoses are not validated. Use laboratory reports, qualified veterinary advice and trusted feed specifications for real decisions. Do not medicate birds based only on this tool.

🥣 Feed nutrient record

Enter values from a laboratory report or a reliable manufacturer specification.

⚖️ Ingredient batch calculator

Calculate ingredient weights from percentages. This does not balance a safe or nutritionally complete ration.

🔬 Microscope image notebook

Store a local preview and label the type of microscope used. Images are not uploaded or analysed by AI.

Image preview stays in this browser session; it is not sent to a server.

🐔 Bird health observation

Prepare clear observations to share with a poultry veterinarian.

Seek urgent veterinary help for sudden deaths, severe breathing difficulty, marked weakness or rapid spread.

📋 Daily flock record

Record production and mortality. Data is saved only in this browser on this device.

💰 Simple farm cost calculator

Enter figures to estimate the difference between sales and recorded costs.
